Who We Are

About HUS MOTORS

HUS MOTORS is an automotive service and motor-spares business offering a focused range of practical vehicle solutions. We sell new and used original motor spares, buy accident-damaged vehicles, rebuild and repair engines, service motorcycles, programme vehicle keys, refuel air-conditioning systems, and charge batteries.


Our goal is to make it easy for vehicle owners to find the parts and services they need through direct, transparent communication. Whether you are a car owner dealing with a mechanical issue, a motorcyclist needing a service, or someone looking to sell a damaged vehicle, HUS MOTORS provides a straightforward way to get the automotive help you need.
